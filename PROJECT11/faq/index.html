Help Center

FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we get the most. Still need help? Contact us →

Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Standard orders are delivered in 2–4 business days across India. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available for select pincodes at ₹149 extra.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! All orders above ₹999 ship for free. Below ₹999, a flat ₹99 shipping fee applies.

Which shipping partners do you use?

We ship via Shiprocket, using partners like Blue Dart, Delhivery, and XpressBees based on your location.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within India only. International shipping is being planned — stay tuned.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We offer 7-day hassle-free returns for unused items in original condition and packaging. Just raise a return request in your account.

How do I exchange for a different size?

Select 'Exchange' when raising a return request. We'll pick up your item and ship the new size within 3–5 business days.

Are there items that can't be returned?

Yes — innerwear, custom/personalised items, and products marked 'Final Sale' cannot be returned or exchanged.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are processed within 2–3 business days after we receive your return. Bank credit can take 5–7 working days.

Sizing

How do I find my size?

Check our Size Guide page for detailed chest, waist, and length measurements. Each product also has fit guidance in its description.

You mentioned 'runs large/runs small' — what does that mean?

We add fit guidance to every product. 'Runs large' means the garment is cut generously — consider sizing down. 'True to size' means standard sizing applies.

I'm between sizes — what should I do?

For a relaxed fit, size up. For a fitted look, size down. Check the product&apos;s specific fit guidance first.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Rupay), Net Banking, Wallets, and Cash on Delivery.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL. We never store your full card details.

Can I use a promo code?

Yes! Enter your promo code at cart or checkout. First-order code: DRIP15 for 15% off.

Account

Do I need an account to order?

No — guest checkout is available. But creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, and manage your wishlist.

How do I track my order?

After placing an order, you'll get an email with a tracking link. You can also track via your Account → Orders page.

Still have questions?

Our team is human, friendly, and quick. We respond within 24 hours.
