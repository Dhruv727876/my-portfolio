Fit Guide

Size Guide

All measurements are in inches. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.

How to Measure

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.

Shoulder

Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the other across the back.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape snug but not tight.

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 8 inches below your waist.

Inseam

Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg along the inner seam.

Length

For tops, measure from the highest shoulder point to the bottom hem.
