Privacy Protocol.

How ELYX orchestrates, protects, and respects your operational intelligence.

Last Updated: Feb 2026

1. Data Sovereignty

At ELYX, we believe your data is yours. We do not sell, rent, or trade your operational data to third parties. We only process the information required to orchestrate your autonomous workflows as defined by your account settings.

2. Zero-Retention Intelligence

Our agents operate on a zero-retention foundation. Unless explicitly configured for long-term memory, the intelligence processed through our reasoning core is purged immediately upon completion of the goal.

3. Enterprise-Grade Encryption

All data ingested by ELYX is encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest and protected by TLS 1.3 in transit. For Enterprise customers, we offer HSM-based key management for complete control.

4. Compliance & Audits

ELYX is SOC2 Type II and GDPR compliant. We maintain full real-time audit logs of all agent actions, accessible through your Master Console at any time.

For specific data requests or deletion, contact our Data Protection Office.
